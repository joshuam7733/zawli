Restaurant operations platform

Zawli

Guests order from a QR code at their table, the kitchen sees every order live, waiters run the floor from their phones, and owners see payments and reports. Many restaurants run on one system, each kept separate.

This is a look-only preview: screenshots of the working app, filled with fictional demo data. A live version you can use is on the way.

The website

Where restaurants learn about Zawli and sign up.

At the table

Guests scan the QR code on their table, browse the menu, add items and send the order from their own phone. Nothing to install.

In the kitchen

New orders appear on the kitchen display the moment they are placed, and tickets can be printed.

On the floor

Waiters follow every order from their phone: what has been served, what is still unpaid, and which allergens to watch for.

For the owner

A dashboard for the day's orders and payments, the full order history, and tables with printable QR codes.
